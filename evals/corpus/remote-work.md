# Northwind Labs Remote and Hybrid Work Guidelines

## Hybrid schedule

Employees assigned to the Bangalore or Berlin office work from the office 2 days per week, on Tuesday and Thursday. The remaining days can be worked from home.

Fully remote arrangements require approval from a VP and are reviewed every year.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible.

## Coworking spaces

Employees who live more than 50 km from an office can be reimbursed up to $200 per month for a coworking space membership.

## Working from another country

You may work from a country other than the one you are employed in for up to 30 days per calendar year. Any longer stay needs approval from HR and Legal because of tax and immigration rules.

## Equipment

Remote employees use the same company laptop as office employees. Home internet costs are not reimbursed.
