# Northwind Labs Information Security Policy

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are changed only when a compromise is suspected; there is no scheduled rotation. Multi-factor authentication is required for every company account. Administrators must use a hardware security key as their second factor.

## Reporting incidents

Report any suspected security incident, such as a phishing email you clicked or malware on your laptop, to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of noticing it.

A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

## Devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption and are enrolled in device management. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access email and chat only after enrolling in device management.

## Data classification

Northwind data has four classes: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details and employee health records, must never be stored on personal devices or shared through personal email. Confidential data may be shared with vendors only under a signed NDA.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's access to production systems every quarter. Access is removed within one business day of an employee leaving the company.
