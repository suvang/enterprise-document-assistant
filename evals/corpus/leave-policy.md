# Northwind Labs Leave Policy

Applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors are covered by the Contractor Guide instead.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 20 vacation days per calendar year. Vacation accrues at 1.67 days per month of service, starting on the first day of employment. Part-time employees accrue vacation in proportion to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31; any carried-over days left after that date are forfeited. Unused vacation is not paid out, except where local law requires it.

Vacation requests of three or more consecutive days must be submitted in the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance and approved by your manager.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Bereavement leave

Employees receive up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member, and 2 days for an extended family member.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the calendar of the office you are assigned to. They do not count against vacation days.
