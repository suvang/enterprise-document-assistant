# Northwind Labs IT Onboarding Guide

## Your laptop

New employees choose between a MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. The laptop is shipped to arrive 5 business days before your start date. If it has not arrived 2 days before you start, contact the IT helpdesk.

## Getting help

Ask the IT helpdesk in the #it-help Slack channel. The helpdesk responds within 4 business hours. Urgent issues that stop you from working can be escalated by phone through the on-call number pinned in the channel.

## Software

Request software through the Software Portal. Common tools such as the code editor, Slack, and the browser are installed automatically. Paid licenses need your manager's approval.

## Network access

Internal tools are only reachable through the company VPN, which uses WireGuard. The VPN client is installed during laptop setup. Do not share your VPN configuration file.

## Accounts

Your single sign-on account is created on your first day. You will be asked to register multi-factor authentication before you can open any other tool.
