# Northwind Labs Expense Policy

This policy covers business expenses paid by employees and later reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the Finance Portal within 30 days of the purchase. An itemized receipt is required for any expense over $25. Expenses submitted after 90 days will not be reimbursed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.

## Approval limits

- Up to $1,000: your manager approves.
- $1,000 to $5,000: a director approves.
- Above $5,000: a VP approves, and Finance must be notified before the purchase.

## Meals

The meal allowance while travelling is up to $75 per day for domestic travel and up to $100 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client dinner.

## Travel

Book flights through the travel tool. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires VP approval.

Hotels are reimbursed up to $250 per night in tier-1 cities and up to $180 per night elsewhere.

## Home office

New employees can claim a one-time home office stipend of up to $500 for a desk, chair, monitor, or similar equipment. The stipend must be claimed within the first 90 days of employment.
