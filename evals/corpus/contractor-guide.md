# Northwind Labs Contractor Guide

This guide covers independent contractors engaged through a statement of work. Contractors are not employees, and the employee Leave Policy and Expense Policy do not apply to them except where stated here.

## Invoicing and payment

Contractors invoice monthly, by the 5th day of the following month. Invoices are paid on net-30 terms.

## Time off

Contractors are not eligible for paid vacation, paid sick leave, or parental leave. Planned absences longer than 3 working days should be agreed with the engagement manager two weeks in advance.

## Expenses

Contractors are not eligible for the home office stipend. Travel requested by Northwind is reimbursed against receipts, with meals capped at $50 per day.

## Devices and access

Contractors normally use their own laptop, which must be enrolled in device management before receiving access to any Northwind system. Contractor access expires automatically at the end date of the statement of work.
